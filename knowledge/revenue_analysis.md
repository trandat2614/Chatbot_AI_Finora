# Phan Tich Doanh Thu (Revenue Analysis)

## Revenue Growth Rate

**Cong thuc:**
```
Revenue Growth Rate = (DT ky hien tai - DT ky truoc) / DT ky truoc x 100
```

**Phan loai:**
- > 20%/nam: Tang truong nhanh
- 10-20%/nam: Tang truong tot
- 0-10%/nam: Tang truong cham
- < 0%: Suy giam

## Average Order Value — AOV

**Cong thuc:**
```
AOV = Tong Doanh Thu / Tong So Don Hang
```

**Chien luoc tang AOV:**
- Bundle san pham (combo)
- Upsell len phien ban cao hon
- Cross-sell san pham bo sung
- Nguong freeship hoac qua tang

## Revenue by Time (Doanh Thu Theo Thoi Gian)

Phan tich doanh thu theo thoi gian giup:
- Xac dinh tinh mua vu (seasonality)
- Phat hien xu huong tang/giam trung han
- Lap ke hoach ton kho va nhan su

**Cac mau thuong gap trong thuong mai dien tu Viet Nam:**
- Cao diem: Thang 1-2 (Tet), thang 6, thang 11-12 (campaign san)
- Thap diem: Thang 3-4, thang 7-8

## Revenue by Product (Doanh Thu Theo San Pham)

Phan tich san pham giup xac dinh:
- **Hero products**: San pham chiem ty trong doanh thu lon nhat
- **Long tail**: San pham ban it nhung co the co margin cao

**Phan tich Pareto (80/20):** Thuong 20% san pham tao ra 80% doanh thu.

## Revenue by Channel (Doanh Thu Theo Kenh)

**Cac kenh pho bien tai Viet Nam:**
- Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Tiki
- Facebook Shop, Instagram
- Website/Zalo OA tu quan
- Offline stores

**Rui ro phu thuoc kenh:** Neu mot kenh chiem > 70% doanh thu, thay doi thuat toan hoac chinh sach san co the anh huong nghiem trong.

## Doanh Thu Tang Nhung Loi Nhuan Giam

Day la truong hop pho bien va nguy hiem. Nguyen nhan co the bao gom:

### 1. Chi Phi Bien Doi Tang Nhanh Hon Doanh Thu
- Gia von hang hoa tang
- Phi van chuyen tang
- Ty le hoan hang (refund) cao

### 2. Chi Phi Marketing Khong Hieu Qua
- ROAS thap du chi nhieu hon
- CAC tang do canh tranh

### 3. Chiet Khau Va Khuyen Mai Qua Nhieu
- Voucher, flash deal lam giam margin

### 4. Phi San Va Chi Phi Van Hanh Tang
- Phi san TMDT tang
- Chi phi logistics tang

**Giai phap:**
1. Phan tich margin tung san pham
2. Toi uu chi phi bien doi
3. Review chinh sach chiet khau
4. Tap trung marketing vao san pham margin cao
